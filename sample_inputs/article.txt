Artificial Intelligence is rapidly
transforming modern education. 
Students can now access 
personalized learning platforms 
that adapt to their pace and 
understanding level.
AI-powered tools help teachers 
automate repetitive tasks such as 
grading and attendance management, 
allowing them to focus more on student 
engagement. However, concerns still exist regarding data privacy,
overdependence on technology, and reduced human interaction in classrooms.
Despite these challenges, AI continues to improve accessibility and learning efficiency for students
around the world.
